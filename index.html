How You'll Learn

The Growth Loop

01 /
Learn Theory
Understand the why — Don't be deceived, the theory is very important
02 /
Try Hands-On
Practice what the textbook says to prove it to yourself
03 /
Get Excited
Get that amazing feeling of seeing what you wire up or build actually work
04 /
Discover Why
Now the textbook makes more sense. Go back and understand what just happened
05 /
Build It Big
Take on a real project — something cool you can use or that solves a problem
06 /
Hit Problems
When they come, don't get discouraged - it's part of the learning process
↻ /
Repeat
Learnt something new? Cool! now try something else, Same loop, harder problems, sharper instincts - that's the way to evel up.
