Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · MechHub NG is operated as a free educational platform for Nigerian mechatronics students.

// Summary

MechHub NG does not sell your data, and does not require an account to access any content. This site is free, educational, and built for Nigerian engineering students.

01 / Who We Are

MechHub NG is a free educational platform designed specifically for Nigerian undergraduate mechatronics engineering students. We provide courses, project guides, resources, and community features to help students learn mechanical design, electronics, and programming. We are not a registered company — this is an independent educational initiative operated by a Nigerian engineering student.

02 / What Data We Collect

We collect minimal data and only what is necessary:

  • → Theme preference — stored locally in your browser via localStorage. Never sent to any server.
  • → Disclaimer dismissal — stored locally in your browser. Never sent to any server.
  • → Anonymous analytics — if enabled in future, will be aggregate only (page views, session duration). No personal identifiers.
  • → Community build submissions — if you submit a build via email or Google Form, your name, school, and build description will be published on the site. You consent to this by submitting.

We do not collect: email addresses (unless you contact us directly), payment information, location data, device fingerprints, or any personally identifiable information without your explicit consent.

03 / YouTube Videos and Third-Party Content

MechHub NG embeds videos from YouTube and links to external resources. We want to be transparent about this:

  • → All videos embedded on this site are created by independent YouTube creators. MechHub NG does not own or produce these videos.
  • → All embedded videos are publicly available on YouTube and are embedded using YouTube's official embed API in accordance with YouTube's Terms of Service.
  • → No videos are downloaded, reproduced, or redistributed. We link to the original source.
  • → When you watch an embedded video, YouTube may collect data according to their own privacy policy. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy.
  • → MechHub NG does not monetise any embedded video content and receives no revenue from video views.
  • → If you are a content creator and believe your content has been embedded inappropriately, contact us at hello@mechhub.ng and we will remove it within 48 hours.
04 / External Links and Resources

MechHub NG links to external websites including YouTube, online stores (Nerdshed, Hub360), and educational platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. Links are provided for educational convenience only. We do not receive affiliate commissions or any compensation for linking to external resources.

08 / Children and Students

MechHub NG is designed for undergraduate university students aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has submitted personal information to this site, contact us at hello@mechhub.ng and we will remove it promptly.

09 / Your Rights

Since we collect almost no personal data, there is very little to exercise rights over. However:

  • → You can clear your localStorage preferences at any time in your browser settings.
  • → If you submitted a community build and want it removed, email hello@mechhub.ng and we will remove it within 48 hours.
  • → If you contacted us by email and want your message deleted, let us know and we will delete it.
10 / Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the site grows and new features are added. The date at the top of this page will always reflect when it was last updated. Continued use of the site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
